Good to know

Where do I find my serial number?
The serial number is printed on your LaptopCare purchase invoice, and it is usually also on the underside of the laptop (look for "SN" or "Serial No."). Both the warranty and service status use the same number.
What does each stage mean?
Warranty: Received → Diagnosis → Parts Ordered → In Repair → Ready for Pickup → Completed.
Service: Processing → Scheduled → Service in Progress → Complete.
If a job is paused for any reason we mark it On Hold and add a note you can read on this page.
How long will my repair take?
It depends on the fault and on parts availability. Once your device reaches our bench we post a note with the estimated completion date on this page, and the status bar moves at every stage.
